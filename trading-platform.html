How it fits this site

Metals and crude futures. The micro contracts for gold (MGC), silver (SIL) and crude (MCL) are a smaller way to practise the markets this desk follows.

The desk’s levels on your own chart. The overnight lows, China open prints and Fibonacci levels in the Asia Overnight and Levels Watch can be drawn on a NinjaTrader chart to see how price behaves around them, in simulation first.

Prop-firm accounts. Many futures prop firms let you trade their evaluation and funded accounts through NinjaTrader. See the prop trading guide for how evaluations work and why most traders fail them.

Add-ons. Trade copiers such as Copilink run inside NinjaTrader 8 to mirror one account to others; check your prop firm’s rules first.
