Getting Paid

Four end-of-day prop accounts, side by side: what you can withdraw, when, how much size you get on day one, and which metals and energy contracts you are actually allowed to trade.

Rules checked 19 October 2026 against the firms’ own help centers · follow-up to My Pick: Rapid EOD 50K

Passing an evaluation is half the job. The other half is the funded rulebook: buffers, caps, minimum withdrawals, a drawdown that locks or keeps trailing, and contract limits that start far below what you had in the evaluation. This page puts the Tradeify Select 50K, 150K and the limited 300K next to the My Funded Futures Rapid EOD 50K, the plan I explained in the previous article.

Where it gets funny

The 300K looks like two 150Ks. It is not. On day one it gives the same 3 minis as a single 150K, and until it is $5,000 in profit it is a 150K-sized account. Two 150Ks would start at 6 minis together. What the 300K does give is room: $8,000 of drawdown behind those 3 minis, against $4,500 on a 150K. On the 150K, the cushion is the bottleneck; on the 300K, size is.

The 300K in one paragraph

A limited release at $349, one time, no subscription; in our test no discount code was accepted. Target $14,000 against $8,000 of drawdown, a better ratio than the 150K’s $9,000 against $4,500. The price for that: a $4,000 daily loss limit the smaller Select sizes do not have, no resets at all, identity verification before purchase, at most three per trader. A funded 300K sits on top of the usual five funded accounts.

The other rules that end accounts

  • End of day is not end of risk. At both firms the floor only moves at the close, but it is enforced in real time, open profit and loss included. Touch it intraday and the account is gone.
  • Flat by the close. Tradeify wants every position closed by 22:45 Brussels time (21:45 in the weeks when only one side of the Atlantic has changed clocks). No overnight holds.
  • No hedging. Tradeify bans opposing positions in correlated products, long gold against short silver included, and across accounts.
  • Hold longer than ten seconds. On Tradeify funded accounts more than half of your trades and of your profit must come from trades held over ten seconds, or no payout.
  • News. Allowed at Tradeify. At MFF allowed in the evaluation, restricted on the funded account.
  • Stay active. Tradeify wants at least one trade a week; MFF applies a seven-day inactivity rule.
