Established dealers, mints, vault platforms and IRA providers, organised by region. Each market works differently, so start with yours.

Last checked 28 September 2026

Physical metal only. This directory covers firms that sell, store or hold actual metal. Financial products such as ETFs and ETCs are a different decision with different risks, and are covered separately in the Commodity ETF Guide.

A directory, not a ranking. Firms are listed for their size, track record or relevance to a market, and no one paid to be included. Order within a region is alphabetical. Sponsored listings, when there are any, are clearly labelled. Always do your own checks before buying or storing metal with anyone.
