Build your own evidence-based thesis

The Gold Investor is not intended to tell readers what they should buy or when they should buy it. It helps them ask better questions, conduct their own research and take responsibility for their decisions.

05

Choosing an instrument

06–09

From timing to purchase

Combine technical timing, risk management, thesis formation and an honest physical purchase.
