Two ways to own it

Physical metal or financial exposure?

Both give you the price of gold. They fail in different ways, so they are covered separately.

Physical metal

Metal you own

  • Bars and coins at home, or allocated in a vault
  • No issuer between you and the metal
  • Costs: premiums, storage, insurance, resale spread
Dealer directory: 300+ firms
Financial exposure

A claim, not a bar

  • ETFs, ETCs and funds, traded like shares
  • Easy sector exposure: metals, energy, grains, softs
  • Costs: fees, roll costs, issuer and custodian risk
