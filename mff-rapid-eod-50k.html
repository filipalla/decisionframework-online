Rapid EOD 50K

Not the best plan for everyone: the best fit for how I trade. A drawdown that only counts the close, daily payouts, and rules that reward the trader who waits for his window.

Prices and rules checked 11 October 2026 · contains a referral link, see the disclosure below

My Funded Futures sells four plans at the 50K size. They all ask the same $3,000 profit target. What separates them is how the drawdown is measured, how fast you can be paid, and what you pay to start. On those three points, Rapid EOD fits how I trade gold, silver and oil. Another trader, with another style or budget, may be better served by another plan; the table further down says which suits whom.

Why this one

  • The drawdown counts the close, not the worst tick. The $2,000 maximum loss trails at the end of the day, in the evaluation and once funded. An intraday swing against you that recovers by the close does not move the floor. On an intraday trailing plan, the same swing can end the account. For metals, where a fix or a data release can shake price $20 before it settles, this is the difference that matters most.
  • No daily loss limit. One bad morning ends the morning, not the day’s plan.
  • Daily payouts once funded. No waiting for a weekly or monthly window.
  • One payment, no activation fee. No monthly subscription running while you wait for the right market.
  • The size fits a balanced position. The plan allows 3 minis or 30 micros. My balanced size, 10 micro gold plus 1 silver, sits comfortably inside it.

The catches

  • The 30% consistency rule. At a $3,000 target no single day may count for more than $900, so you cannot pass on one big CPI day. With 4 minimum days, plan on at least four steady sessions. I see this as a feature: it is how I want to trade anyway.
  • The $2,100 cushion before the first payout. “Daily payouts” start only after you have built that cushion in the funded account.
  • End of day cuts both ways. The floor ignores intraday dips, but it also trails up on every good close. Bank distance before adding size.
  • News once funded. Tier-1 releases are restricted on the funded account. Know the calendar; the Session Clock lists every US data slot in Brussels time.
